# LockerEasy – Video Outline  
_Concise, professional structure for a 5-minute final video._

---

## 1) Introduction – Stage 1 (0:00–1:00)

### Objective
Introduce the project context and the origin of LockerEasy.

### Key Points
- Presenter introduction (Nico).  
- Purpose of the video.  
- Initial problem: manual locker rental process.  
- Identification of flaws and customer needs.  
- First user flows, objectives, and acceptance criteria.  
- Core concept: a fast, clear, configurable system.

### Suggested Visuals
- Early diagrams.  
- Initial backlog items.  
- Notes and first design sketches.

---

## 2) Development – Stage 2 (1:00–2:30)

### Objective
Show how complexity increased and how the team adapted.

### Key Points
- Redefining tasks and adjusting previous work.  
- Requirement changes and team reorganization.  
- Building stronger technical models and class relationships.  
- Prioritizing realistic implementation within time constraints.  
- Focus on clarity, scope, and team limitations.

### Suggested Visuals
- UML diagrams.  
- Whiteboard with workflows and lists.  
- Repository clips and technical documentation.

### Testimony Inserts
Short team audio clips (5–7 seconds):  
- “The hardest part was adapting…”  
- “I felt pressure because of exams…”

---

## 3) Project Closure – Stage 3 (2:30–4:30)

### Objective
Highlight the emotional and human side of the project: pressure, difficulties, and technical decisions.

### Key Points
- Combined pressure: deadlines, exams, and failing code.  
- Constant errors in both UI and backend.  
- Emotional load due to UI depending on the presenter.  
- Moments of doubt (“I thought we wouldn’t finish”).  
- Key technical decisions:
  - Simplifying the system.  
  - Rebuilding broken controllers.  
  - Limiting scope to essential features.  
  - Documenting each fix to avoid setbacks.  
- The turning point: starting the UI from scratch, slowly and calmly.  
- Outcome: a more mature and aware team.

### Suggested Visuals
- Controllers, JSON files, interface clips.  
- Console errors.  
- UI rebuild progress.

### Testimony Inserts
Short emotional audio segments from teammates.

---

## 4) Conclusion (4:30–5:00)

### Objective
Close the video with a clear, professional, optimistic message.

### Key Points
- Main lessons learned through the three stages.  
- Importance of communication and realistic decision-making.  
- Team readiness for the full programming stage.  
- Final thanks.

### Suggested Visuals
- Medium shot of the presenter.  
- LockerEasy logo on screen.

---
