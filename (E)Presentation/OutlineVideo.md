# Video Outline – LockerEasy:

## 1) Introduction (0:00–0:40)

- **Purpose:** Welcome the audience. Briefly introduce LockerEasy and Don David’s business.
- **Key idea:** “This stage was not only about features, but about new challenges and team learning.”
- **Visual:** Presenter (Nico) on camera, friendly and honest.

---

## 2) Main Challenges and Problems (0:40–1:30)

- **Focus:** Show the real problems: changing requirements, difficulty to organize, a team member leaving.
- **Visual:** Presenter gives a short intro, then clips of team members answering:
    - What was the most difficult part of this stage?
    - How did changes in scope affect your work?
    - How did you feel when a member left?
    - 
- **Note:** Let each team member talk for 15–20 seconds. Use 2–3 answers per question.

---

## 3) How We Regrouped (Replanteamiento) (1:30–2:10)

- **Focus:** How the team responded to problems. The regrouping process, prioritizing, and rethinking the plan.
- **Visual:** Presenter describes a team meeting, shows a list or diagram of priorities, maybe footage of discussion.
- **Tip:** Mention a real decision or turning point for extra detail.

---

## 4) Team Changes (2:10–2:40)

- **Focus:** The impact of one member leaving and a new member joining. How this changed team dynamics.
- **Visual:** Short comments from the new member:
    - How was it to join the team?
    - What did you find hardest at the start?
    - What can you bring to the team?
- **Note:** The new member can have a longer answer to give depth.

---

## 5) The Process of Deciding UML Models (2:40–3:50)

- **Focus:** Explain the **process** (not the code) used to decide what UML models/classes to create and why.
    - Analyze what the client (Don David) really needs.
    - Decide what is possible to do this sprint.
    - Team shares ideas, reviews, and checks if each model is really useful.
    - Use interfaces for future flexibility.
    - Show and confirm the UML diagram with Don David and the team.
- **Visual:** Presenter on camera, shows the UML diagram, and maybe group discussing.
- **Tip:** Presenter can repeat key points, show steps visually, or ask a member for a short comment.

---

## 6) What We Learned and Next Steps (3:50–4:50)

- **Focus:** Reflection on teamwork, process over code, and growth.
- **Visual:** Presenter gives a summary, team members share (10–15 seconds each) something learned or a goal for the next stage.
- **Tip:** Add a motivational note about moving from planning to coding.

---

## 7) Conclusion (4:50–5:20)

- **Message:** Thank the audience, final thoughts, and say the team is ready to start coding for Don David’s business.
- **Visual:** LockerEasy logo, presenter waving goodbye, soft music.

---

## 8) Credits (optional, 5–10 s)

- Names and roles of the team, music in the background.
