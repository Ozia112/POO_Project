# Video Production Guidelines

**Objective**:

- Honestly share the main challenges the team faced in this stage of the LockerEasy project.
- Show how the team regrouped, adapted requirements, and grew stronger—even without much technical progress.
- Briefly remind the context (Don David, lockers/printing business), but focus on teamwork, learning, and readiness for the next step.

**Tone & Style**:

- Honest, close, and reflective; keep it real, but with optimism.
- **First-person** narration (Nico), but now including real voices of team members (short interviews).
- Language: everyday, simple, clear (B1 English), avoiding technical jargon.

**Narrative**:

- Structure in 4 blocks: **Introduction → Team interviews (problems) → Presenter wraps up (actions & lessons) → Goodbye**.
- Center the message on overcoming problems, adapting as a team, and preparing for the programming stage.

**Visual Resources**:

- **Interviews** at the table (one-on-one, presenter and team member).
- Presenter at the desk for intro/wrap-up.
- Use existing storyboard images; keep scenes clean and minimal.

**Filming**:

- Desk and table shots, with both wide and close-ups.
- Soft, even lighting. Background should be clean (no need for special effects or overlays).
- Record interviews with each team member sitting across from the presenter.

**Music**:

- Light, soft background music. Lower volume when someone speaks.
- Use the same style of music as previous videos for consistency.

**Other Notes**:

- Keep it short and honest—focus on team experience more than technical progress.
- Show the team’s human side: reactions, emotions, and lessons learned.
- End on a hopeful note about starting programming with a stronger team.
