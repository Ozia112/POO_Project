# Video Production Guidelines

**Objective**
- Clearly and warmly explain how **LockerEasy** addresses Don David’s context (printing/lockers) with a **configurable template**.  
- Mantra: **“two taps and done.”**  
- No code and no jargon.

**Tone & Style**
- Close, direct, with light humor.
- **First-person** narration (Nico), formal/everyday language.

**Narrative**
- Structure in 5 blocks: **Introduction → What we did → How we did it → What’s next → Conclusion**.
- Keep messages short and a single throughline: “speed and simplicity for people in a hurry.”

**Visual Resources**
- **Whiteboard** to reinforce ideas (no complex overlays).

**Filming**
- Medium desk shot.
- Clean background and soft lighting (no technical specs needed).

**Music**
- Light, discreet, and continuous. Lower it during narrated parts.
