###System Requirements: Financial

###Funcional Requirements
1. The system must be able to automatically generate a detailed log of all transactions performed throughout the day. This includes a breakdown of total income, number of operations, type of services purchased, and any pending or failed payments.
Users must have access to an interface where they can view both the daily summary and the historical list of transactions filtered by date, service type, or transaction category. The system should keep this information organized, searchable, and available for future audits.
