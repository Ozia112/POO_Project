# User Stories

## Administrator

### User Story - Manage Prices and Rental Rates and Products
**As an Administrator**, I want to **manage the prices for locker rentals and products** to **adjust the rates according to the business needs**.
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - The administrator can input and update rental and product prices from the administration panel.
  - The changes should be reflected in real-time in the system.

### User Story - Generate and View Sales and Revenue Reports
**As an Administrator**, I want to **generate and view daily sales and revenue reports** to **have control over the system’s financial performance**.
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - The system must allow the administrator to generate reports in Excel or PDF format.
  - The reports must provide detailed information about sales and rentals.

### User Story - Manage Product Catalog
**As an Administrator**, I want to **manage the product catalog** to **add or remove products available for sale**.
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - The administrator can add new products to the catalog.
  - The administrator can remove products from the sales catalog.

### User Story - Register Locker Rentals with Details (Customer, Duration, Cost)
**As an Administrator**, I want to **register a locker rental** to **assign it to a customer with the correct details (customer, duration, cost)**.
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - The system must allow the administrator to register the locker rental with the customer's details and duration.
  - The system should automatically calculate the rental cost.

### User Story - End Rental and Mark Locker as Available
**As an Administrator**, I want to **end a locker rental and mark it as available** so that **other customers can rent it**.
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - The system must allow the administrator to end a rental and immediately mark the locker as available for the next customer.

### User Story - Cancel Rental
**As an Administrator**, I want to **cancel an active rental** to **free the locker for another customer**.
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - The system must allow the administrator to cancel an active rental and immediately free up the locker.

### User Story - Apply Promotional Discount to Rental
**As an Administrator**, I want to **apply a promotional discount to a locker rental** to **offer better prices to customers**.
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - The system must allow the administrator to apply promotional discounts to locker rentals.

### User Story - Record Product Sales
**As an Administrator**, I want to **record product sales** to **link the sale to customers and their locker rentals**.
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - The system must allow the administrator to record the sale of products and link the sale to a customer and their locker rental.

### User Story - Link Product Sales to Customers with Locker Rentals
**As an Administrator**, I want to **link product sales to customers who have rented a locker** to **connect both actions**.
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - The system must allow the administrator to link product sales to customers who have rented a locker.

### User Story - Apply Discount to Product Sales
**As an Administrator**, I want to **apply a discount to product sales** to **reduce the cost of purchases for customers**.
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - The system must allow the administrator to apply discounts to product sales.

## User (General Employee)

### User Story - Register Locker Rentals with Details (Customer, Duration, Cost)
**As a User**, I want to **register a locker rental** to **assign it to a customer with the correct details (customer, duration, cost)**.
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - The system must allow the user to register the locker rental with the customer's details and duration.
  - The system should automatically calculate the rental cost.

### User Story - Extend Rental Time (Up to 15 Minutes at No Extra Charge)
**As a User**, I want to **extend the rental time of a locker** for up to 15 minutes without additional charge to **give more flexibility to customers**.
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - The system must allow extending the rental time of a locker without additional charge, up to a maximum of 15 minutes.

### User Story - End Rental and Mark Locker as Available
**As a User**, I want to **end the rental and mark the locker as available** so that **other customers can rent it**.
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - The system must allow the user to end the rental and immediately free up the locker.

### User Story - Cancel Rental
**As a User**, I want to **cancel an active rental** to **free the locker and make it available for another customer**.
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - The system must allow the user to cancel an active rental and immediately free up the locker.

### User Story - Apply Promotional Discount to Rental
**As a User**, I want to **apply a promotional discount to a rental** to **offer better prices to customers**.
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - The system must allow the user to apply promotional discounts to locker rentals.

### User Story - Record Product Sales
**As a User**, I want to **register the sale of products** to **link the sale to the locker rental of the customer**.
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - The system must allow the user to register the sale of products and link it to a customer’s locker rental.

### User Story - Apply Discount to Product Sales
**As a User**, I want to **apply a discount to product sales** to **reduce the total cost of the purchase**.
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - The system must allow the user to apply discounts to product sales during the checkout process.
