# General Competences

**Teamwork**: In our team we maintain a relationship of trust between us and constant support to address the complications that we encounter with the project.

**Communication**: In our team we maintain a constant conversation about progress and problems in the project with the idea that we are all informed.

**Critical thinking**: Each member is able to give and receive constructive criticism on the work of the others in order to achieve a better result as a team.

**Time organization**: As a team we organize with exact metrics the time lapses of each part of the work we do.
