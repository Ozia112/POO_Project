# Log

The log contain the tracker of reunion and a little explication about each one

## Types of reunion

**Sprint Planning**: in this reunion the team planning the objetive and tasks in the backlog, organization of work and what each member going to work in the next days until de sprint review, every reunion take a 2-3 hour to finished and can take 1-2 days depend of the objetive of the sprint

**Sprint Review**: After the two weeks of work, the team have three reunion:

- (Team): Our team take a reunion of 2-3 hours to finished his work and/or to solicitude pull request to the departament branch to finish merge to the branch "stage #".
- (Client): The team and client meet to talk about the work advanced and the team hear the feedback of the client
- (Stake Holder Academic): The SHA check the advaced of the project to take calification and same make a feedback about the 

**Sprint Retrospective**: Finally after the all the reunion of Sprint Review the team talk about our the information recolected and take notes about what we have to do in the next Sprint Planning.

**Daily Scrum**: This reunion have the funtion to talk and make questions about the task that the team are doing and check how we advace. Every reunion don't take more of 15 minutes and could be do it in presential or virtual in an online comunication media channel (Discord, Whatsapp).
