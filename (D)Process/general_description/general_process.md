# 📌 General Process Description — LockerEasy

The **LockerEasy** team works under the **Scrum** framework, which allows for a clear organization of roles, responsibilities, and the way project activities are structured and documented.

## Team Organization  

The team consists of 5 members with defined roles:  

- **Product Owner (internal):** Represents the interests of the professor and client (main Stakeholder) and the project principles.  
- **Scrum Master:** Facilitates Scrum events, ensures proper application of the framework, and oversees process quality.  
- **Developers:** Execute technical activities, select PBIs during Sprint Planning, and implement functionalities.  
- **Department Leads:** Generate, organize, and oversee the activities related to their area (Product, Requirements, Process, Presentation, Competencies).  

The professor acts as the **academic Stakeholder**, reviewing the project and setting deadlines, while the client serves as the **main Stakeholder**, participating in Sprint Reviews to evaluate progress but not in Sprint Planning.  

## Roles and Responsibilities  

- **Product Owner (PO):** Prioritizes Product Backlog Items and represents Stakeholder needs.  
- **Scrum Master (SM):** Ensures compliance with the agile framework and supports the team in removing impediments.  
- **Developers:** Select and execute tasks, creating Pull Requests into the corresponding department branch.  
- **Department Leads:** Responsible for generating activities with clear criteria and following up on their department backlog.

## Management Tool  

Backlog and activities are managed in **GitHub Projects**, with the following states:  

- **Backlog:** Pool of PBIs generated by department leads.  
- **ToDo:** Tasks approved during Sprint Planning and assigned to a Developer.  
- **InProgress:** Tasks under active development.  
- **InReview:** Tasks with an open Pull Request to the department branch.  
- **Done:** Tasks merged into the delivery branch.
