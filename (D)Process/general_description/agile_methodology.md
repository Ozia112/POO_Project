# 📌 Adopted Agile Methodology — LockerEasy

The team adopted the **Scrum** framework, adapted to a school project.  

## Scrum Events

1. **Sprint Planning**  
   - Define Sprint objectives and select tasks from the backlog.  

2. **Daily Scrum**  
   - Daily meeting for Developers to synchronize progress.  

3. **Sprint Review**  
   - Presentation of the work to the **client (main Stakeholder)** and academic review by the **professor (academic Stakeholder)**, along with evaluation by department leads and the Scrum Master.  

4. **Sprint Retrospective**  
   - Analyze the Sprint to detect improvements for the next iteration.  

## School Adaptation  

- The **professor** participates as the **academic Stakeholder**, reviewing the project and setting deadlines, but not intervening in Sprint Planning.  
- The **client** acts as the **main Stakeholder**, validating progress during Sprint Reviews.  
- The **internal Product Owner** represents the interests of both the professor and client within the team.  
- The **Scrum Master** facilitates events and ensures compliance with the framework.  
- The **Developers** select tasks during Sprint Planning and work independently on implementation.  

## Management Tool  

The team uses **GitHub Projects** with the following board states:  

- **Backlog** → Pool of PBIs created by department leads.  
- **ToDo** → Tasks approved in Sprint Planning and assigned to a Developer.  
- **InProgress** → Tasks in active development.  
- **InReview** → Tasks with an open PR to the department branch.  
- **Done** → Tasks merged into the delivery branch.
