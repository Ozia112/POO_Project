# **Product Description**

## **Objective**

An efficient locker rental and product sales manager designed to optimize registration and simplify the accounting process. The platform is intended to be intuitive, allowing users to register sales and manage services efficiently, with a clear focus on automating repetitive tasks and providing adequate visual support for ease of use.

---

## **Problem Statement**

| **Problem**                                     | **Impact**                                                      |
|-------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------|
| Manual management of locker rentals             | Operational inefficiencies, human errors, slow processes.      |
| Manual calculation of rental times              | Increased error margin and time spent on operational tasks.   |
| Lack of adequate tools (e.g., Excel)            | Difficulties in tracking transactions.                        |

**Consequence:** Increased time spent on repetitive tasks and human errors in the administrative process.

---

## **Scope**

### Locker Rental Management

- **Locker Rental:** Management of locker rentals in a single location.
- **Simple Interface:** Locker clients are automatically integrated into the sales system.

### Sales Management

- **Independent Tab:** Product sales are handled in a separate section.
- **Adjustments:** The system allows adjustments like cancellations, rental time extensions, and discounts.

---

## **Limitations**

- **Offline:** The application works without a constant internet connection, designed for internal administration.
- **Simplified Interface:** Limited functionality to avoid overwhelming the user.
